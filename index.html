About

Bridging stories across two worlds

A cultural translation studio bridging children's books between Korea and the Netherlands & Belgium.

We don't just translate words — we carry what AI can't: the breath of the story. The rhythm, the warmth, the cultural nuance that makes a book come alive for young readers on both sides of the world.

📍 Based in Antwerp — a city woven into the history of European publishing, from the UNESCO-listed Plantin-Moretus printing house to today's renowned Flemish picture-book tradition. We work from the heart of Europe's children's book culture.
